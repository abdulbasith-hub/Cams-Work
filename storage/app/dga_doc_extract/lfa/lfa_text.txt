Chapter3:LocalFundAuditDepartment
Establishment
The Local Fund Audit Department began functioning as a separate department as per G.O.Ms.No.609, Finance Department, dated 22.03.1880. Subsequently, the department was placed under the administrative control of the District Collector as per G.O.Ms.No.514, Finance Department, dated 10.03.1885, and functioned under such control from 1885 to 1920.(Paragraph 3.1.1)
Basedontherecommendationsofanexpertcommitteeappointedin1920,thedepartmentwasbroughtunder the administrative control of the Finance Department through G.O.Ms.No.125, Finance Department, dated 10.02.1921. Thereafter, theExaminer of Local Fund Accounts was appointed as theHead of Department as per G.O.Ms.No.1016, Finance Department, dated 27.11.1922.(Paragraph 3.1.2)
As per G.O.Ms.No.848, Finance (LF) Department, dated 09.11.1995, the nomenclature of the department wasrevised—thedesignation'Examiner'waschangedto'Director',andthedepartmentwasrenamedasthe Directorate of Local Fund Audit. Subsequently, all Audit Departments functioning under the Finance Department, including the Local Fund Audit Department, were brought under the administrative control of the newly created post of Director General of Audit, as per G.O.(Ms).No.102, Finance (LF) Department, dated07.04.2022.TheDirectorGeneralofAuditisdesignatedastheex-officioDirectorofLocalFundAudit under Section 3 of the Local Fund Audit Act, 2014.(Paragraph 3.1.3)
